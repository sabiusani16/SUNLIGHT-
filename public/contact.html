Contact

We are happy to help. The fastest way to reach the SUNLIGHT team is through the app, where you can follow the status of your request and read our reply.

Already have an account?

Open the app, go to Settings → Help → Support and choose Contact Admin, Report a Problem, Request Help or Account Support. You can attach a screenshot.

Open SUNLIGHT

Forgot your password?

Passwords can only be reset by the team. Contact us and we will confirm the account is yours before issuing a new password.

Reporting abuse

To report a user or a message, open the chat, tap the menu and choose Report. Your identity is never shown to the person you report.
