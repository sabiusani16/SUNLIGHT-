Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains in plain language what SUNLIGHT collects and how it is used. It is a general template written from how the app works today; the SUNLIGHT owner should review it (and have it checked by a lawyer if required in your country) before relying on it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, username and a password. Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are kept only as salted hashes.
  • Optional contact details: a phone number or email address, if you add one. Only you and the SUNLIGHT administrators can see these.
  • Your content: messages, voice notes, photos, videos, statuses, group and channel posts that you send or share.
  • Support and reports: messages you send to support, and reports you submit about other users, including any screenshots you attach.
  • Technical data: sign-in sessions, last-seen time, and basic error logs needed to keep the service running.

2. How we use it

  • To run SUNLIGHT: deliver your messages and calls, show your contacts your name, photo and online status.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, deal with abuse and suspend accounts that break the rules.
  • To give you support and answer your requests.
  • To fix errors and improve reliability.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your private conversations are visible to the people taking part in them. Group and channel content is visible to its members.
  • SUNLIGHT administrators can access the information needed to provide support, handle reports and keep the service safe.
  • When you report someone, the person you reported is never told who reported them.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Service providers

SUNLIGHT runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database and file storage). If email verification is enabled, verification emails are sent through an email delivery provider.

5. Keeping your data

We keep your information while your account exists. Statuses expire automatically. If you delete your account, your account data, your support requests and your uploaded files are removed.

6. Your choices

  • You can delete your account from Settings → Account.
  • You can block users and report abuse from any chat.
  • Your username, password, phone number and email are locked once set to protect your account. To correct one, contact support.

7. Security

We use protections such as hashed passwords, private sign-in sessions and server-side permission checks. No online service can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong, unique password and never share it.

8. Children

SUNLIGHT is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Visit our Contact page.
