Install

Put SUNLIGHT on your home screen

SUNLIGHT opens in your browser. Add a shortcut so it is one tap away, just like any other app.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open SUNLIGHT in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open SUNLIGHT in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is SUNLIGHT free to use?

You can create an account and start chatting from the app. If anything about the service changes, we will say so inside the app.

Do I need to download anything?

No. SUNLIGHT runs in your browser. On your phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app (see Install above).

How do I sign up?

Open the app, choose “Create account”, enter your name, a username and a password. Then add friends by their username.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

For your safety, passwords can only be reset by the SUNLIGHT team. Contact support and we will verify that the account is yours before giving you a new password.

Can I change my username, phone number or email?

Your username and password are locked once set. You can add a phone number or email once if you have not added them yet. After that, only the SUNLIGHT team can change them, so your account cannot be taken over easily.

How do I report someone or ask for help?

Open a chat, tap the menu and choose Report. For anything else, go to Settings → Help → Support and message the team. You will see their reply and the status of your request.

Will the person I report know it was me?

No. Reports go only to the SUNLIGHT administrators. The reported person is never told who reported them.

Which browsers work best?

A recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Calls and voice notes need permission to use your microphone (and camera for video).
